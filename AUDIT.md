## major SITE-07 At least 7 links and buttons on 2 pages have nothing for a screen reader to say

**Why it matters.** A blind visitor hears only "link" or "button", with no idea where it goes or what it does. Accessibility audits flag it under WCAG 4.1.2 \(Name, Role, Value\), the standard that laws such as the ADA and the European Accessibility Act are commonly measured by; an automated read shows a likely barrier, not a legal finding, so a person confirms each one.

**Likely fix.** Give each one words: visible text, an aria-label, or an alt on the image inside it \(an icon link to a cart: aria-label="Cart"\).

## minor SITE-24 4 security headers no page sends

**Why it matters.** Each header closes off one way the site could be misused \(a downgrade to http, a script slipped in, the site framed in another to trick a click\). A missing one is a weaker defence, not a sign the site has been attacked; security reviews ask for them, and each costs a line of server configuration.

**Likely fix.** Add the missing headers in the web server or the CDN \(one setting each\), starting with Strict-Transport-Security.

<details open>
<summary>4 missing headers</summary>

- `/` no page of the site sends Strict-Transport-Security \(without it, browsers are not told to keep to https\)
- `/` no page of the site sends Content-Security-Policy \(without it, nothing limits which scripts a page may run\)
- `/` no page of the site sends X-Frame-Options or frame-ancestors \(without it, another site can frame the page to trick a click\)
- `/` no page of the site sends X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff \(without it, a browser may guess a file's type from its content\)

</details>
