About Us Video
Read the video transcript

At Frontline Insurance, we help home and business owners protect what matters most: their homes, businesses and the lives they have built. For more than 25 years, we have built our reputation helping protect coastal communities through dependable homeowners insurance and commercial property insurance. As we continue to grow into new states and communities, that experience remains the foundation of how we serve every customer, combining practical support, responsive service and smarter ways to help people prepare for the unexpected.

Compassion is part of who we are. We are motivated by people, not paperwork, but by the opportunity to make things better. From helping customers understand and reduce risks to supporting them after a loss, Frontline is here to help prevent the bad and protect the good.

We believe insurance should feel personal. That means making thoughtful decisions, finding new ways to help and doing the right thing because it is the right thing to do. Through innovative digital tools or personal support from our team, we keep looking for better ways to connect with customers and be there when they need us most.

We are more than an insurance company. We are people helping people protect what they have worked hard to build, today and whatever may come next.
