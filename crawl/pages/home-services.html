Home Services

Losses happen, and when they do, we are here for you and your insurance needs.

With Home Solutions and Home Checkup, Frontline is doing more than just insuring property. We're a partner in protecting the health of your home.

Home Checkup

Getting a Frontline Home Checkup is the first step in helping ensure your home is protected for years to come. Depending on your home and policy, Frontline may require an inspection called a Home Checkup.

  • Option 1: Self-Guided Checkup

    If your home is eligible for Home Checkup by CHRP, you’ll receive instructions to complete a simple mobile-friendly inspection on your own schedule – without an inspector entering your home.

  • Option 2: In-Person Checkup

    Otherwise, a qualified Frontline partner will visit your home to document important exterior and, when applicable, interior features of your home.

Home Solutions

  • Introducing Home Solutions

    At Frontline, we believe the best claim is the one you never have to make, so we created Home Solutions to help homeowners improve their home’s health.
