Frontline Insurance has a new look. After 28 years of protecting homes and families across the southeast and mid-Atlantic, we felt it was time for a brand that better reflects who we've become, while staying true to who we've always been.

What hasn't changed is everything that matters: your policy, your coverage, your agent, and the team that answers when you call. This new brand changes how we look - not how we work, and not the promises we've made to you.

On this page you'll find our story, our promises to you, and the same trusted products you already rely on. Thank you for being part of the Frontline family. Together, we're ready for whatever comes next.

Standing Strong Since 1998.

  1. 1998

    In the wake of Hurricane Andrew, Frontline was established in Florida with a mission to protect property owners in some of the country's most challenging coastal markets.

  2. 2008

    Building on a decade of experience in Florida, Frontline expanded into South Carolina, bringing our approach to property protection to more coastal communities.

  3. 2013

    Frontline expanded into Alabama and North Carolina, extending our footprint further across the Southeast.

  4. 2019

    In Georgia, Frontline continued its steady expansion while building on more than two decades of property insurance experience.

  5. 2022

    Frontline surpassed $1 billion in in-force premium and stood strong through 20+ storms, including Hurricane Ian, one of the most significant hurricanes in Florida's history.

  6. 2024/26

    Expansion into Virginia, Maryland and Tennessee, marked an important step in Frontline's evolution from our coastal roots toward becoming a broader regional property carrier.

  7. Future Ready (2026 & Beyond)

    A new brand for the road ahead. Frontline's new look reflects how far we've come and where we're going - while the stability, trust, and commitment behind our name remain.

Same Products You Trust

  • Homeowners Insurance

    Coverage for owner-occupied, seasonal, and secondary homes, helping protect your property, belongings, and peace of mind from unexpected events.

  • Condo Insurance

    Protection designed for condominium owners, with coverage options for your unit, personal belongings, liability, and additional living expenses.

  • High Value Homes

    Comprehensive protection for higher-value homes, including coastal and inland properties, with coverage tailored to unique needs and assets.

  • Stepdown Deductible

    A unique program that rewards claim-free policyholders with the opportunity to earn cash back on eligible hurricane, windstorm, or hail deductibles.

  • Reciprocal Exchange

    Insurance coverage built for homeowners seeking strong protection through a member-focused approach designed to support policyholder needs.

  • Builder's Risk

    Protection for properties under construction, covering structures, materials, and equipment throughout the building process.

  • Rental Properties

    Insurance designed to help protect your investment property, with customizable coverage options for rental homes and associated risks.

  • Flood Insurance

    Responsive flood coverage that helps protect your property from flood-related damage, with excess flood options available in select areas.

  • Commercial Property

    Coverage designed for businesses, helping protect commercial buildings and supporting business continuity when unexpected losses occur.

  • Wind & Hail

    Coverage that helps protect homes from wind and hail damage, whether the property is your primary residence or a rental home.
