Introducing FrontlineFlex™

November 3rd, 2026

Your home is unique. Your insurance coverage should reflect that. At Frontline Insurance, we are always innovating to make things better. That is why we are introducing FrontlineFlex™, a new homeowners insurance policy designed to give eligible Florida homeowners more flexibility when it matters most. Initially available for Florida HO3 and DP3 policies, FrontlineFlex offers two optional endorsements, Stepdown Deductible® and Deductible Eliminator, that can help reduce out-of-pocket expenses following eligible covered losses. 

What is FrontlineFlex? 

FrontlineFlex is a homeowners insurance policy that offers additional deductible benefits through optional endorsements. From hurricane damage to water damage and other covered property losses, homeowners insurance deductibles can represent a significant expense. FrontlineFlex provides two ways to help manage those potential costs. 

Stepdown Deductible®: Make Claim-Free Years Count 

Florida homeowners know the importance of preparing for hurricane season. With Stepdown Deductible, consecutive claim-free years can lead to increasing potential savings on your hurricane deductible. 

Starting in year one, eligible homeowners may receive a cash-back benefit following a covered hurricane claim. That benefit increases with each consecutive claim-free year, reaching up to 100% in year six. Filing a hurricane claim does not reset the Stepdown Deductible benefit level. 

Learn more: Step Into Savings: Understanding Frontline’s Stepdown Deductible Policy 

Deductible Eliminator: Reduce Out-of-Pocket Expenses 

Not every covered loss is caused by a hurricane. Water damage and other unexpected events can also leave homeowners responsible for deductible expenses. 

For an additional premium, the FrontlineFlex Deductible Eliminator endorsement may reimburse the applicable All Other Perils (AOP) or Water Loss Deductible after an eligible covered loss exceeds the deductible, subject to the policy and endorsement terms. 

Plan now. Save more later. Deductible Eliminator offers another way to prepare financially for unexpected property damage. 

Can you choose both FrontlineFlex endorsements? 

Yes. Subject to policy eligibility and availability, eligible Florida HO3 and DP3 policyholders can select Stepdown Deductible, Deductible Eliminator, or both. Each endorsement addresses different deductible expenses, allowing homeowners to explore options based on their coverage needs. 

What Current Stepdown Policyholders Should Know 

Policyholders who currently have Stepdown coverage will be offered the Stepdown coverage option under FrontlineFlex when their policy renews. Their accumulated Stepdown benefit percentage will remain the same, and additional information about available options will be included with the renewal offer. 

Protecting When it Matters Most 

At Frontline Insurance, we are committed to developing innovative insurance solutions that meet the evolving needs of homeowners. FrontlineFlex builds on that commitment by offering added flexibility and deductible benefits designed with eligible Florida homeowners in mind.
